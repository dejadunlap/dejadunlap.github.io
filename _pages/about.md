Hi! My name is Deja Davis (formerly Deja Dunlap). I am a PhD Student at the University of California, Berkeley in the Information Sciences Department, where I am co-advised by Professors Diag Davenport and Diedre Mulligan. My current research interests are concerning fairness in AI. More broadly, I'm interested in studying minority communities relationship with the development, deployment, and usage of emerging technologies from a legal, sociologal, and technical viewpoint.

Outside of my research, I'm extremely passionate about addressing educational inequalities, particular for minority students in STEM. I have worked in numerous roles facilitating increased access to educational and professional experiences for minorities students, and plan to continue this work as a further in my academic career.

I received my B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Yale University.
